3. AI processing

Practice content is processed by third-party AI providers via the OpenRouter router to produce estimated feedback. Current sub-processors are listed in our vendor register; the active model routes are recorded per release. Speech-to-text runs locally on our server — no third party receives raw audio. Your content is not used to train models.

AI band scores are estimates and are not official IELTS results.

5. Your rights

You may request access/export, correction, deletion and (where consent is the basis) withdrawal. In the app: Settings → Export, Settings → Delete account, or profile editing. You can also email [PRIVACY@CONTACT]; we verify identity in-app before sensitive operations.

6. Cookies

Strictly necessary only: an opaque session cookie and a CSRF cookie. No advertising or cross-site tracking.

7. International transfers

AI sub-processors may process content outside your country; regions and transfer bases are recorded per release. We apply the strictest available retention/training opt-outs at the router.
