# 00 — Recommended Strategy

## Final Recommendation

### DECISION — [High confidence]

Launch **ARUORA IELTS in Indonesia as a free, goal-driven preparation platform**, but treat free access as a **data + network acquisition strategy**, not as the permanent business model.

Do not launch JLPT yet.

Do not launch a tutor marketplace yet.

Do not build a social feed.

Do not make WhatsApp a critical dependency.

---

# 1. Beachhead

## IELTS first

Primary target:

1. Scholarship hunters
2. Study-abroad applicants
3. IELTS retakers stuck around a target threshold
4. Young professionals with an overseas requirement

Why IELTS first:

- existing pilot already contains the learning engine
- user outcomes are measurable
- deadline and target score are explicit
- high-cost test creates real motivation
- lecturer cohort is available
- market learning can later inform JLPT architecture

---

# 2. Product Thesis

ARUORA should evolve from:

> AI IELTS Coach

into:

> **IELTS Readiness Network**

Core promise:

```text
Where am I?
↓
What is my target?
↓
What is holding me back?
↓
What should I do today?
↓
Who can help when self-study is not enough?
```

---

# 3. Differentiation

Do **not** lead with:

- AI
- adaptive learning
- mock test
- personalized dashboard

These are increasingly commodity features.

Lead with:

### Destination-Aware Readiness
Goal + deadline + target + current gap.

### Actionable Diagnosis
Not more content; next best action.

### Learner Graph
Understand users by level, target, weakness, deadline, availability and behavior.

### Relevant Peer Network
Match people who are actually useful to each other.

### Human Escalation
AI/self-study → peer → mentor/tutor when needed.

### Outcome-to-Supply Loop
Successful learners can later become verified contributors, mentors or tutors.

---

# 4. Launch Sequence

## Wave 0 — Internal QA
2–5 users.

Exit:
- P0 = 0
- P1 = 0

## Wave 1 — Lecturer Controlled UAT
Recommended: 20–50 students.

Goal:
- usability
- stability
- placement completion
- learning-flow comprehension
- pedagogical review

Do not use retention here as pure PMF evidence because participation is externally prompted.

## Wave 2 — LinkedIn Market UAT
Recommended: 30–50 qualified users.

Goal:
- voluntary demand
- organic onboarding completion
- meaningful learning
- return behavior
- peer-interest signal

## Wave 3 — Free Founding Beta
Recommended: 100–300 qualified/active learners.

Goal:
- learner density
- retention baseline
- manual Study Pool
- validate social learning demand

## Wave 4 — Community Beta
Only after Study Pool demand is observed.

Goal:
- Study Pods
- peer speaking
- structured peer review
- reputation

## Wave 5 — Human Help Pilot
5–10 carefully selected mentors/tutors.

Goal:
- willingness to book
- price sensitivity
- impact on plateau
- repeat session behavior

---

# 5. Free Access Policy

### DECISION — [Medium-high confidence]

Founding Beta core learning access should be free.

But “free” does not mean technically unlimited.

Use:
- pooled/pre-generated content where possible
- generation caps
- caching
- model routing
- rate limits
- costly AI only when pedagogically valuable

Public message:

> **Free during Founding Beta**

Avoid promising:

> Free forever.

---

# 6. Acquisition

Priority:

1. Lecturer partnership
2. Founder-led LinkedIn
3. Referral
4. Campus / scholarship / English communities
5. Organic content
6. Paid ads only after activation + retention baseline exists

---

# 7. Community

Start with:

> **Study Pool**

Opt-in collection of learners open to peer practice.

Then manually create:

> **Study Pods**

4–8 learners.

Do not build a feed.

---

# 8. WhatsApp

### DECISION — [High confidence]

Do not make Aura WhatsApp bot required for initial UAT.

Reason:
- core product must prove itself independently
- WhatsApp API economics are changing on 1 October 2026
- service replies that are free today are scheduled to become billable per message
- WhatsApp can later be valuable for re-engagement and matching, but it should not own the core learning journey

First use:
- optional reminder
- re-engagement
- placement-result notification
- Study Pod invitation

- follower system
