# 04 — Positioning & Value Proposition

## Category

Do not frame ARUORA purely as:

> Language Learning App

Recommended category:

> **IELTS Readiness & Preparation Network**

---

# Positioning Statement

For Indonesian learners preparing IELTS for scholarship, overseas study or career goals, **ARUORA** is a goal-driven preparation platform that diagnoses the gap between current performance and target readiness, recommends what to train next, and connects learners with relevant peers or human support when self-study is not enough.

Unlike generic resource libraries, social groups, or standalone AI scorers, ARUORA combines:

- target
- deadline
- performance history
- personalized training
- learner matching
- escalation

into one journey.

# Do Not Overclaim

Avoid:

- “official IELTS score”
- “guaranteed band”
- “examiner-level AI”
- “guaranteed scholarship”
- “pass in 30 days”

Use:

- estimated band
- readiness estimate
- practice performance
- gap to target
- recommended focus
