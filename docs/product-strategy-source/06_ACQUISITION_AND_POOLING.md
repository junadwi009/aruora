# 06 — Acquisition & User Pooling

## Goal

Not maximum signup.

Goal:

> **High-density pool of qualified IELTS learners.**

---

# Phase 1 Channels

## 1. Lecturer Partnership
Use for controlled testing.

Value:
- rapid cohort
- structured feedback
- low acquisition cost
- academic reviewer access

Limitation:
- participation bias

---

## 2. LinkedIn Founder-Led Acquisition
Use for market validation.

Why:
- scholarship and professional audience fit
- founder story can build trust
- qualified UAT can be manually screened

CTA:

> **Join ARUORA IELTS Founding UAT**

Not:

> “Please test my application.”

---

# LinkedIn Content Sequence

## Post A — Founder Story + Recruitment
Problem → product → Founding UAT.

## Post B — Product Learning
Show placement → gap → plan.

## Post C — Early Insight
Share anonymized learning from UAT.

No fake claims.

---

# Phase 2 Channels

After retention signal:
- university English clubs
- scholarship communities
- alumni communities
- campus international offices
- referral
- creator partnerships

---

# Paid Ads

### DECISION

Do not prioritize paid ads before:

- activation baseline
- D7 baseline
- first meaningful-session conversion
- clear target segment
- landing message validated

Otherwise ads buy churn.

---

# Free Founding Beta

Public framing:

> **Free during Founding Beta**

User gives value back through:
- usage data
- structured feedback
- optional interview
- optional peer participation

Do not force positive reviews.

---

# Founding Learner Identity

Badge:

> **Founding Learner · 2026**

Possible future role path:
- learner
- achiever
- peer contributor
- mentor
- tutor

---

# Referral

Avoid generic coin referral.

Use utility:

> “Your Study Pod needs two more learners.”

Referral reward later may include:
- extra review
- deeper diagnostic
- priority beta access

Only if economics allow.

# Pool Quality Metric

Track:

> **Qualified Active Learner Density**

Not just registered pool size.

Example:
“How many active learners exist in a matchable cluster?”

A cluster may be:
- target 7.0
- current 5.5–6.5
- Writing/Speaking focus
- exam within 90 days
- evening availability
