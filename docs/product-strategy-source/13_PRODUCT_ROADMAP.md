# 13 — Product Roadmap

## Stage 0 — Stabilize Pilot

Focus:
- reliability
- scoring disclaimers
- telemetry
- auth
- responsive UX

Do not add major social features.

---

# Stage 1 — ARUORA Rebrand + Controlled UAT

Add:
- brand identity
- Aura visual integration
- goal/destination/deadline capture
- cohort/source tracking
- improved result hierarchy

Run lecturer cohort.

---

# Stage 2 — Market UAT

Recruit through LinkedIn.

Add only fixes that improve:
- activation
- placement
- first value
- first meaningful session
- return

---

# Stage 3 — Free Founding Beta

Target:
100–300 qualified active learners.

Add:
- Founding Learner status
- Study Pool opt-in
- richer readiness
- progressive profiling
- referral tracking

No full community yet.

---

# Stage 4 — Manual Study Pods

Operate manually.

Measure:
- acceptance
- participation
- repeat
- retention
- usefulness

---

# Stage 5 — Community Beta

Only if manual Pods work.

Build:
- pod management
- peer scheduling
- peer review
- reputation
- moderation

---

# Stage 6 — Human Feedback Pilot

5–10 people.

Potential products:
- Writing review
- Speaking feedback
- targeted diagnostic session

---

# Stage 7 — Tutor Supply System

Build:
- verification
- mentor history
- tutor calibration
- booking
- tutor learner brief

---

# Stage 8 — Monetization

Choose based on observed willingness:
- premium intelligence
- expert review
- tutoring
- intensive cohort

# Stage 9 — JLPT Discovery

Only after IELTS engine/network mechanics are proven.

Run separate market discovery.
