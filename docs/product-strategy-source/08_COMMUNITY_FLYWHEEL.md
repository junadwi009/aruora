# 08 — Community Flywheel

## Community Is Not a Feed

ARUORA community exists to help a learner progress.

Core units:

- Study Pool
- Study Pod
- peer review
- speaking partner
- mentor
- alumni/achiever

---

# Cohort Lifecycle

```text
Target / Exam Cohort
        ↓
Active Preparation
        ↓
Exam / Outcome
        ↓
Did not reach target? → Next Attempt
Reached target?       → Verified Achiever
```

Community should expect cohorts to expire.

Do not rely on one permanent group staying active forever.

---

# Role Evolution

```text
Learner
↓
Active Contributor
↓
Verified Achiever
↓
Peer Mentor
↓
Tutor Candidate
↓
ARUORA Tutor
```

---

# Why This Matters

Exam-prep products naturally lose users after success.

ARUORA can turn success into:

> **supply**

Possible supply:
- peer mentor
- tutor
- Study Pod host
- scholarship mentor
- alumni contributor
- content collaborator

---

# Reputation

Do not use popularity metrics as primary status.

Prefer:
- verified result
- useful reviews
- peer session reliability
- response quality
- mentorship history
- moderation history

Avoid:
- follower count
- likes as expertise
- raw activity = authority

# Community Safety

Before public matching:

Required:
- report
- block
- opt-in
- moderation rules
- privacy defaults
- no automatic exposure of private phone number
- clear peer-vs-tutor labels

Initial recommendation:

> Keep Study Pods inside ARUORA or a controlled platform.

Do not automatically create WhatsApp groups that expose user phone numbers.
