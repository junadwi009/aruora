# 03 — Target Segments & Jobs-to-be-Done

Segment by **job-to-be-done**, not demographics alone.

---

# Segment A — Scholarship Hunter

## Trigger
Needs language requirement for scholarship/application.

## Core job
> “Help me become eligible before the deadline.”

## Anxiety
- score requirement
- deadline
- application complexity
- retest cost
- uncertainty about readiness

## Product priority
- target/deadline
- readiness
- Writing/Speaking diagnosis
- focused training
- application-calendar awareness later

## Community potential
High.

Shared target + deadline creates natural cohorts.

---

# Segment B — Overseas Degree Applicant

## Trigger
University application / admission requirement.

## Core job
> “Tell me what score I need and whether I am on track.”

## Product priority
- destination
- university target
- exam timeline
- readiness
- study plan

## Community potential
Medium-high.

---

# Segment C — IELTS Retaker / Plateau Learner

## Trigger
Previous result below requirement.

Common pattern:
- 0.5–1.0 band gap
- one skill blocks the overall requirement

## Core job
> “Tell me why I am stuck and what will actually move the score.”

## Product priority
- previous score import
- skill diagnosis
- mistake taxonomy
- plateau detection
- human escalation

## Monetization potential
Potentially high, but must be validated.

---

# Segment D — Young Professional

## Trigger
Work, licensing, migration or overseas career.

## Core job
> “Help me hit the requirement without wasting my limited time.”

## Product priority
- efficient schedule
- target per skill
- deadline
- mobile use
- tutor escalation when time is short

---

# Initial Priority Ranking

1. Scholarship Hunter
2. Retaker / Plateau Learner
3. Overseas Degree Applicant
4. Young Professional

Reason:

Scholarship users create acquisition clusters; retakers create strong diagnostic value.

---

# Behavioral Data to Capture

Minimum:

```text
goal
destination_country
target_band
target_per_skill
exam_date
application_deadline
previous_test_taken
previous_score
weekly_study_capacity
weakest_self_reported_skill
preferred_study_time
peer_practice_opt_in
```

Do not ask everything in one onboarding screen.

Use progressive profiling.

---

# Core User Question

ARUORA should answer:

> **“Given my target, deadline and current performance, what is the highest-value thing I can do next?”**
