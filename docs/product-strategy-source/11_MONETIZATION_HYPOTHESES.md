# 11 — Monetization Hypotheses

## Phase 1

### DECISION
Do not optimize revenue during UAT / early Founding Beta.

Primary objective:
- activation
- retention
- learning value
- user density
- first-party behavior data

---

# Free Founding Beta

Recommended:
- placement
- Reading/Listening
- core Writing/Speaking practice
- basic feedback
- plan
- progress
- Study Pool/Pods later

Cost controls still apply.

---

# Monetization Hypothesis A — Premium Intelligence

Potential later paid layer:
- deeper diagnostic
- more AI review
- longitudinal analytics
- intensive exam-plan mode

Risk:
official/free competitor tools already offer AI and personalization.

Therefore this cannot be assumed to be the strongest revenue line.

---

# Monetization Hypothesis B — Human Feedback

Potential:
- Writing expert review
- Speaking review
- targeted consultation
- tutor session

This is strategically stronger for plateau/high-deadline users.

Must validate willingness to pay.

---

# Monetization Hypothesis C — Tutor Marketplace Take Rate

Only after:
- tutor demand
- reliable supply
- repeat bookings
- platform value prevents leakage

Do not assume marketplace unit economics in advance.

---

# Monetization Hypothesis D — Cohort / Premium Program

Later:
- 30/60/90-day intensive cohort
- AI + Study Pod + tutor checkpoint

Potentially aligned with scholarship deadlines.

Requires operational validation.

---

# Monetization Hypothesis E — Institution

Future:
- university/lecturer dashboard
- cohort analytics
- campus preparation program

Do not build until consumer workflow is proven.

---

# Willingness-to-Pay Research

During UAT ask behaviorally:

- Have you paid for IELTS preparation before?
- What did you buy?
- What problem was worth paying for?
- Would you pay for a human Writing review?
- Would you pay to avoid an unnecessary retake?
- What would make a tutor session worth it?

Do not rely only on:
> “How much would you pay?”
