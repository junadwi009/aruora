# 01 — Market Evidence

## 1. Scholarship Demand

### VERIFIED

LPDP reported **79,126 applicants in 2025**, with **4,211 people passing** that year's scholarship selection.

Implication:

- scholarship aspiration is a large high-intent funnel
- the majority of applicants do not become awardees in that cycle
- users may have repeated preparation/application journeys

Important:

> LPDP applicants are **not equivalent to IELTS users**.

Some programs/destinations may not require IELTS, and some applicants target domestic programs.

Use LPDP only as a **demand proxy for scholarship-driven preparation**, not IELTS TAM.

---

## 2. 2026 Continued Scholarship Demand

### VERIFIED

LPDP's 2026 Batch I press release reported **32,794 applicants** and **2,753 participants passing the substantive selection**.

Implication:

The scholarship funnel remains active in 2026 and provides a recurring acquisition calendar.

---

## 3. Outbound Study Demand

### VERIFIED

Australia's DFAT reported that Australia remained the **number-one study destination for Indonesian students**, with a record **29,672 enrolments in September 2025**.

Implication:

Destination-based onboarding is strategically meaningful.

ARUORA should ask not only:

> “What IELTS score do you want?”

but also:

> “What are you trying to reach?”

---

## 4. IELTS Test Economics

### VERIFIED

British Council Indonesia lists IELTS Academic and General Training at approximately **Rp3.6 million** in current Indonesian-language material.

IDP Indonesia similarly lists an approximate regular IELTS fee around **Rp3.6 million**.

Implication:

A failed or unnecessary retake has meaningful financial cost.

Potential value proposition:

> Improve readiness before spending on another official attempt.

Do not imply ARUORA can replace the official test.

---

## 5. LinkedIn Reach

### VERIFIED

DataReportal's Digital 2026 Indonesia report cites LinkedIn advertising resources showing **37.0 million registered members in Indonesia in late 2025**.

Important caveat:

This is registered-member reach data, **not monthly active users**.

Implication:

LinkedIn is a sufficiently large channel to test founder-led acquisition among students and professionals, but reach alone does not establish conversion quality.

---

## 6. Official IELTS Prep Is Already Strong

### VERIFIED

British Council's current IELTS Ready offering includes free features such as:

- mini mock assessment
- AI feedback
- estimated band/readiness feedback
- full mock tests
- guided modules

IELTS Ready Premium adds:

- 40 full practice tests
- AI feedback
- personalized dashboard
- study recommendations
- progress tracking
- self-study material
- webinars

Implication:

> **AI + personalized IELTS preparation is not a durable differentiator on its own.**

ARUORA must build value above content/scoring.

---

## 7. Peer Learning Evidence

### VERIFIED

A 2025 meta-analysis of **26 experimental studies** and **190 effect sizes** found a large favorable average effect of peer tutoring on L2 writing performance (**Hedges' g = 0.84**).

A separate meta-analysis on L2 peer feedback also found a significant positive overall effect.

Implication:

Structured peer learning is pedagogically plausible.

However:

- research does not prove ARUORA Study Pods will automatically work
- peer systems need structure and prompts
- peer feedback must not be presented as official IELTS scoring

---

## 8. Learner Frustration / Plateau Signals

### COMMUNITY SIGNAL

Recent public IELTS discussions repeatedly contain behaviors such as:

- being stuck around Writing 6.0–6.5
- having a 0.5-band gap
- exam/visa deadlines moving closer
- uncertainty about whether AI feedback is trustworthy
- asking whether to self-study, take a general course, or hire a tutor

These discussions are anecdotal.

They support a hypothesis:

> A high-intent ARUORA segment may be learners who know their target but cannot diagnose the bottleneck.

They do **not** establish prevalence.

---

## 9. Future JLPT Opportunity

### VERIFIED — future only

Japan Foundation's 2024 survey reported **732,914 Japanese-language learners in Indonesia**, the second-largest total among countries/regions in its ranking.

Official JLPT statistics show **34,353 Indonesian applicants** for the December 2025 JLPT sitting, with the largest level volume at N4.

Implication:

JLPT remains a credible future expansion opportunity.

### DECISION

Do not build JLPT now.

First prove:
- activation
- retention
- readiness logic
- matching
- community

with IELTS.
