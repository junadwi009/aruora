# 09 — Tutor Supply Strategy

## Core Rule

> **High score does not equal teaching ability.**

ARUORA must not convert users directly from achiever to paid tutor.

---

# Supply Ladder

## Learner
Normal user.

## Verified Achiever
Achievement verified through evidence accepted by platform policy.

## Peer Contributor
Provides useful peer interactions.

## Peer Mentor
Meets reliability and quality thresholds.

## Tutor Candidate
Applies / is invited into teaching calibration.

## ARUORA Tutor
Passed teaching-quality checks.

## Senior Tutor
Later role; may supervise/calibrate.

---

# Candidate Signals

ARUORA can eventually observe:

- score history
- verified outcome
- peer review usefulness
- session reliability
- communication rating
- strongest skill
- learner level they help most
- moderation record

This may create stronger internal recruitment evidence than a cold CV alone.

---

# Tutor Validation Before Marketplace

Do not build open marketplace first.

Pilot:

5–10 tutors.

Offer:
- Writing review
- Speaking feedback
- plateau consultation

Track:
- booking intent
- booking conversion
- repeat booking
- outcome
- tutor rating
- time-to-feedback
- off-platform leakage

---

# Human Escalation Logic

Possible product logic:

```text
AI/self-study
↓
No improvement / high uncertainty
↓
Peer support
↓
Still stuck OR deadline high-risk
↓
Qualified human
```

Aura can recommend:

> “This looks like a good point for human feedback.”

Not:
> “You must buy a tutor.”

---

# Marketplace Moat Hypothesis

ARUORA can know:

- exact gap
- recent mistakes
- study history
- deadline
- target
- plateau duration

Therefore a tutor can receive a structured learner brief before session.

Potential differentiation:

> **diagnosis-driven tutor matching**

rather than tutor browsing.

---

# Monetization Integrity

Never intentionally make free AI worse merely to push tutoring.

Human help should be offered where it plausibly adds value.
