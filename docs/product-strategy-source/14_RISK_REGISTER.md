# Primary Strategic Risks

## 1. Commodity AI
This is the biggest positioning risk.

## 2. False Network Thesis
Users may say they want peers but not actually interact.

Manual Pods must validate behavior before engineering investment.

## 3. Trust
Wrong/misleading score feedback can damage the entire brand.

## 4. Founder Overbuilding
ARUORA has a large long-term vision.

The first proof is much smaller:

> Can qualified IELTS learners get value, return, and benefit from relevant peer/human support?
