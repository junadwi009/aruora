# Experiments

## E1 — Goal-first vs score-first onboarding
Question:
Does goal/destination increase completion and perceived relevance?

## E2 — Readiness vs streak hierarchy
Question:
Does readiness-focused home increase meaningful sessions?

## E3 — Study Pool interest
Question:
Do users opt into relevant peer learning?

## E4 — Manual Study Pod
Question:
Does a Pod increase D7/D30 and practice completion?

## E5 — Plateau escalation
Question:
When shown targeted human help, do stuck learners engage?

## E6 — WhatsApp opt-in
Question:
Do learners actually want Aura on WhatsApp, and does it increase return enough to justify messaging cost?

# Analytics Integrity

Never optimize only for:
- time in app
- messages sent
- streak length

If longer time does not correspond to learning value, it is not success.
