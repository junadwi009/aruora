# 04 — Aura Character Guide

## 1. Character Role

**Name:** Aura  
**Role:** ARUORA product mascot and AI companion  
**Identity:** female chibi scholar / nerd  
**Universe:** ARUSTUDIO visual universe  
**Relationship:** product-specific companion; not the parent studio mascot

Aura is not a teacher.

Aura is not a pet.

Aura is:

> **A quiet, intelligent companion who understands the learner's journey.**

---

# 2. Core Visual Description

Aura adalah perempuan chibi bergaya 3D minimalis dengan kesan:

- nerdy
- calm
- intelligent
- slightly aloof
- quietly cute
- premium collectible

Visual Aura harus terasa seperti karakter designer toy atau soft 3D figurine, bukan anime character dan bukan mascot vector.

---

# 3. Proportion

Recommended:

- head large and dominant
- body short and simplified
- compact silhouette
- stable full-body readability at small size

Approximate direction:

```text
Head visual mass: 50–55%
Body visual mass: 45–50%
```

Aura boleh sedikit lebih compact daripada Aru, tetapi jangan ekstrem chibi hingga infant-like.

---

# 4. Face

Signature expression:

- calm
- half-lidded / relaxed
- observant
- mildly unimpressed

Avoid:

- huge sparkling anime eyes
- exaggerated blush
- permanent smile
- puppy expression
- hyper-cute open mouth

Face should communicate:

> “I noticed that.”

---

# 5. Eyes

Preferred:

- dark simple shapes
- slightly half-lidded
- clean, minimal geometry

Emotion may be shown through:

- eyelid
- slight gaze direction
- subtle brow change
- glasses position

---

# 6. Hair

Signature:

- black / near-black bob
- straight blunt bangs
- clean rounded outline
- slight inward curve at ends

Hair is one of Aura's strongest silhouette identifiers.

Do not frequently change hairstyle.

---

# 7. Glasses

Signature accessory:

- large round glasses
- thin dark frame
- clean minimal bridge

Glasses represent:

- intelligence
- study
- curiosity
- quiet nerdiness

Do not remove glasses from the default/base model.

---

# 8. Horns

Aura memiliki dua small luminous horns.

Difference from Aru:

- Aru: physical horns
- Aura: light / aurora horns

Properties:

- small
- soft
- translucent
- lavender-white glow
- non-aggressive

Avoid demon aesthetics.

---

# 9. Base Outfit

Current direction:

- cream / off-white
- oversized tunic / dress / top
- simple rounded silhouette
- soft fabric
- minimal branding

Optional:

- small crossbody bag
- subtle ARUORA mark
- Spark symbol

Avoid:

- school-uniform cliché
- graduation gown
- wizard robe
- heavy techwear
- overly cute pastel fashion

---

# 11. Aura Ribbon

Aura may be accompanied by a thin aurora ribbon / glow.

Purpose:

- presence
- motion
- AI listening
- progress
- emotion
- transition

The ribbon must be subtle.

It must not permanently dominate the frame.

---

# 12. Expression Set

Minimum production set:

1. Neutral
2. Curious
3. Thinking
4. Listening
5. Correct
6. Excellent
7. Not Quite
8. Repeated Mistake
9. Concerned
10. Proud
11. Sleep / Idle
12. Welcome Back
13. Community Match Found
14. Tutor Escalation
15. Achievement

---

# 13. Pose Set

Minimum:

- standing neutral
- holding book/tablet
- adjusting glasses
- sitting and reading
- listening
- presenting result
- pointing to readiness
- holding checklist
- looking at progress
- waving subtly
- sleeping at desk
- holding small Spark
- beside Study Pod
- beside tutor card

---

# 14. Aura Core / Spark

Aura may use a small `✦` motif as a recurring symbolic element.

Can appear:

- bag
- UI near Aura
- achievement
- loading
- notification icon

Do not place too many sparks around every render.

---

# 15. Rendering Style

Preferred:

- soft 3D
- matte / soft vinyl feel
- smooth shadows
- minimal material complexity
- neutral background
- warm studio lighting
- restrained bloom/glow

Avoid:

- photoreal skin
- sharp anime linework
- glossy plastic overload
- detailed eyelashes
- realistic human anatomy
- over-rendered fantasy VFX

---

# 16. Character Design Rule

Aura must remain recognizable in:

- monochrome silhouette
- 64px UI avatar
- sticker
- full 3D render
- social media image
- loading animation

Recognition should come primarily from:

1. bob haircut
2. round glasses
3. calm eyes
4. light horns
5. cream outfit

---

# 17. Mascot Usage

Good:

- onboarding guide
- placement result
- AI chat
- reminder
- readiness summary
- peer matching
- tutor recommendation
- achievement
- empty state
- WhatsApp identity

Avoid:

- on every card
- repeating same pose 10 times per screen
- replacing functional iconography
- appearing during serious error/security messages unless appropriate

---

# 18. Narrative

Keep lore minimal.

Primary narrative:

> **Aura grows brighter as you move closer to your goal.**

Do not create long fantasy lore unless ARUORA later intentionally develops narrative IP.
